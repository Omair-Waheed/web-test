Privacy Policy

1867 Royal Canadian Army Cadet Corps recognizes the importance of protecting the privacy and personal information of cadets, families, instructors, volunteers and visitors.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how 1867 Royal Canadian Army Cadet Corps (1867 RCACC) approaches the collection, use, storage and disclosure of personal information in connection with this website and its public communications.

We recognize our responsibility to respect the privacy of the young people and adults who participate in, support or interact with our corps.

This policy is intended to complement, and not replace, applicable Canadian Armed Forces Cadet Program privacy requirements, Army Cadet League policies or other applicable organizational requirements.

2. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to information handled in connection with the public-facing 1867 RCACC website and its associated public communications.

Certain information relating to enrolment, training, administration and participation in the Cadet Program may be collected and managed by the Canadian Armed Forces, the Cadet Leagues or other authorized organizations under their own applicable policies and procedures.

This website is not intended to replace official Cadet Program registration, administration or communication systems.

3. Information We May Collect

The information we handle depends on how individuals interact with the corps.

Information Provided Through Direct Contact

When someone contacts us by email or another communication method, we may receive information such as:

  • Name and contact information.
  • Email address.
  • The contents of messages and enquiries.
  • Other information voluntarily provided.

Website Technical Information

When visitors access this website, certain technical information may be processed by the website hosting provider, such as information associated with website requests and browser connections.

The website may also use browser-local storage to remember a visitor's light or dark theme preference. This preference is stored in the visitor's browser and is not intended to identify the visitor.

Information We Do Not Intentionally Request Through This Website

This website is not intended to collect sensitive personal information, medical information, enrolment records or other confidential Cadet Program information.

Please do not submit sensitive personal or administrative information through general website enquiries.

4. How Information Is Used

Information provided directly to the corps may be used for reasonable purposes associated with responding to enquiries and supporting communication with cadets, parents, guardians and community members.

Personal information will not knowingly be used for unrelated purposes or disclosed to other parties without appropriate authorization or another applicable legal basis.

5. Photographs and Video

Photographs and video recordings are an important way of documenting corps activities, recognizing achievements and sharing the experiences of our cadets and staff.

However, we recognize that photographs and recordings can contain identifiable personal information, particularly when they feature children and young people.

Cadets Under 18

Photographs and videos identifying cadets will only be published through public-facing corps communications when appropriate authorization has been obtained and documented in accordance with applicable Cadet Program and organizational requirements.

Where parental or guardian authorization is required, it must be obtained before publication. The wishes and privacy concerns of the cadet should also be respected.

Authorization to participate in the Cadet Program should not automatically be treated as permission for every possible use of photographs, videos or personal information.

Adult Instructors and Volunteers

Instructors, volunteers and other adults may also appear in photographs and recordings published on this website.

Appropriate permission should be obtained for identifiable photographs and videos of adults used in public-facing communications, subject to applicable organizational requirements.

Use of Photographs and Recordings

Authorized photographs and recordings may be used for purposes such as:

  • Documenting corps activities and events.
  • Recognizing cadet achievements.
  • Sharing corps news and announcements.
  • Promoting the Army Cadet Program.
  • Providing information about corps activities.

We will take reasonable steps to avoid publishing unnecessary personal details alongside photographs and recordings.

Requests Regarding Published Images

Cadets, parents, guardians, instructors and volunteers may contact the corps to raise concerns about photographs or recordings published on this website.

Requests for removal or review will be considered and addressed as appropriate, taking into account applicable authorization, organizational requirements and legal obligations.

6. Disclosure of Information

We do not intend to sell or rent personal information collected through our public communications.

Information may be shared with authorized individuals or organizations where necessary for legitimate organizational purposes, where appropriate consent has been obtained, or where disclosure is otherwise permitted or required by law.

Photographs and other material published on this website may be accessible to members of the public and may be copied or shared by third parties.

7. Third-Party Websites and Services

This website may contain links to external websites and services, including official Canadian Armed Forces Cadet Program resources and registration systems.

When visitors follow these links, they leave the 1867 RCACC website and become subject to the privacy practices and policies of the external service.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of external websites.

Our website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Website hosting and infrastructure services may process technical information in accordance with their own applicable privacy policies.

8. Information Security and Retention

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards should be used to protect personal information handled by the corps against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or loss.

Personal information should only be retained for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable legal and organizational requirements.

Access to personal information should be restricted to individuals who require it for their authorized responsibilities.

9. Access, Corrections and Privacy Concerns

Individuals may contact the corps to ask questions about personal information handled in connection with the website or to raise concerns about its collection, use or disclosure.

Subject to applicable law and organizational responsibilities, individuals may request:

  • Information about how their personal information is handled.
  • Access to personal information held about them.
  • Corrections to inaccurate information.
  • Review of consent relating to photographs or recordings.
  • Withdrawal of consent where applicable.

Requests involving official Cadet Program records may need to be directed to the appropriate Canadian Armed Forces or Cadet League representative.
