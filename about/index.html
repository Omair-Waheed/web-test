About 1867 RCACC

Proudly serving the North Delta community, 1867 Royal Canadian Army Cadet Corps provides opportunities for young people to learn, develop and grow.

Who We Are

1867 Royal Canadian Army Cadet Corps is a youth organization based in North Delta, British Columbia.

Through the Canadian Cadet Program, we provide young people with opportunities to develop leadership, citizenship, teamwork, confidence and personal skills.

Our corps is associated with the Seaforth Highlanders of Canada, reflecting a connection to Canada's military heritage and traditions.
