Volunteering

Our corps benefits from the time, experience and dedication of volunteers who help support cadets and their activities.

Make a Difference

Volunteers help strengthen the community surrounding the corps and contribute to the success of various activities and initiatives.

Whether you have a few hours to spare or would like to become more involved, there may be opportunities to contribute.

Volunteer Opportunities

Parent Committee

Assist with the civilian organization supporting the corps, including meetings, planning and administrative activities.

Fundraising

Help organize and support fundraising activities and community initiatives.

Special Events

Assist with approved community events, ceremonies, special activities and other organizational requirements.

Administrative Support

Contribute organizational, communication or administrative skills to support committee activities.

Community Outreach

Help develop relationships with local organizations, businesses and community supporters.

Volunteer Requirements

Certain volunteer activities may require authorization, screening, training or other procedures before participation.

Individuals working directly with cadets or participating in activities involving youth must follow the applicable Canadian Armed Forces Cadet Program and sponsoring organization requirements.

Please contact the committee before assuming that a particular volunteer activity is open to all participants.

Interested in Volunteering?

We'd be happy to hear from individuals interested in contributing their time and skills.

Contact us to discuss available opportunities and any applicable requirements.
