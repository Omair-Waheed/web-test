Training & Activities

Discover the training activities, experiences and opportunities available to cadets.

About Cadet Training

Training is an important part of the Army Cadet Program. Through regular activities and practical experiences, cadets develop knowledge, skills and confidence.

Activities are designed to encourage personal development, teamwork, leadership and responsible citizenship.

Training Activities

Leadership

Cadets develop leadership skills through instruction, practical activities and opportunities to accept responsibility.

Citizenship

Activities encourage community involvement, responsible citizenship and an understanding of the responsibilities of being an active member of society.

Physical Activities

Physical activities promote fitness, teamwork and personal well-being.

Field Training

Field activities provide opportunities to apply knowledge and skills in practical outdoor environments.

Drill & Ceremonial

Cadets develop discipline, coordination and teamwork through drill and ceremonial activities.

General Military Knowledge

Cadets learn about military history, traditions, organizations and related subjects as part of their program.
