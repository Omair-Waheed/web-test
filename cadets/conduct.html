Conduct & Expectations

Being a cadet means demonstrating respect, responsibility, teamwork and good citizenship.

Our Expectations

Every cadet contributes to the environment and culture of the corps.

Cadets are expected to conduct themselves responsibly, treat others with respect and contribute positively to activities and training.

Respect

Cadets are expected to treat fellow cadets, instructors, volunteers and members of the community with courtesy and respect.

  • Respect others regardless of background or experience.
  • Listen when others are speaking.
  • Use appropriate and respectful language.
  • Help maintain an inclusive environment.
  • Resolve disagreements appropriately.

Responsibility

Cadets are responsible for their own actions, preparation and participation.

  • Arrive prepared for scheduled activities.
  • Follow appropriate instructions.
  • Take care of issued equipment and uniforms.
  • Communicate when assistance is needed.
  • Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

Teamwork & Leadership

The Cadet Program encourages cooperation and leadership at every level.

Cadets should support one another, contribute to group activities and demonstrate positive leadership through their actions.

Safety

Safety is an important responsibility during all corps activities.

  • Follow applicable safety instructions.
  • Use equipment only as instructed and authorized.
  • Report hazards, injuries and unsafe situations.
  • Ask for assistance whenever necessary.

Cadets should never hesitate to raise a safety concern with an appropriate member of staff.

Online Behaviour

Cadets should demonstrate the same respect and responsibility online as they do during in-person activities.

  • Communicate respectfully.
  • Respect other people's privacy.
  • Do not share confidential corps information.
  • Do not publish photographs of others without appropriate permission.
  • Follow applicable online safety requirements.

Questions or Concerns?

Cadets who have questions, concerns or need assistance should speak with an appropriate member of corps staff.

Concerns involving safety, harassment, bullying or inappropriate behaviour should be raised through the appropriate official reporting channels.
